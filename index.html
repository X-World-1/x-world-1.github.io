A controllable multi-camera world model.

Generate future driving observations from multi-view history and ego actions, with optional control over the scene and its appearance.

01OVERVIEW

From actions to future observations.

X-World models how a driving scene evolves under a specified action sequence. It generates synchronized multi-camera video directly in observation space.

Observe
A short history from seven synchronized cameras provides the visual context.
Condition
Specify future ego motion. Add traffic agents, road elements or appearance prompts when needed.
Generate
Predict the corresponding multi-camera future, with joint modeling across views and time.

03METHOD

One model, multiple conditions.

A multi-view latent video generator, built on WAN 2.2 5B, combines visual history with distinct control signals.

01 / ATTENTION

Connect views and time

Alternating attention across cameras and timesteps models shared geometry, object identity and motion.

02 / CONDITIONING

Keep controls distinct

Ego actions use adaptive normalization; camera parameters use additive embeddings. Separate cross-attention branches handle dynamic agents, static elements and text.

03 / REPRESENTATION

Work in video latent space

A 3D causal VAE encodes video into a compact representation. A multi-view DiT generates future latents that are decoded back into camera observations.

From short clips to streaming generation

STAGE I

Learn controllable multi-view generation

Bidirectional training on synchronized short clips establishes action following, scene control and cross-view modeling.

Bidirectional generation · typically ~50 denoising steps

STAGE II

Generate the future chunk by chunk

A causal model uses self-forcing and distribution matching distillation. A rolling KV cache carries recent context into subsequent chunks.

Causal generation · 4 denoising steps per chunk
